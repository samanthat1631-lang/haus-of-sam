The story behind the Haus

Personal beginnings.
Purposeful design.

Haus of Sam began as a personal creative project and grew into a design studio centered on sports, celebration and life's meaningful moments. The store was originally named after Flash, the corgi who appeared on the first golf ball design.

Today, Haus of Sam brings together customizable gifts and keepsakes designed to spark joy, honor milestones and make everyday moments feel a little more special.
